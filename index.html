01 Privacy Commitment & Scope

This Privacy Policy governs the collection, processing, and protection of information by the Giovnex mobile application ("the Application"), developed and operated by Giovane Bang-ay ("the Developer"). Giovnex is engineered as an independent, high-performance browser and utility application designed with a foundational commitment to user data privacy.

Binding Guarantee: Giovnex does not sell, lease, commercialize, or trade personal browsing histories, search queries, or user file contents to third-party data brokers or marketing networks.

02 Telemetry, Diagnostics & Security Integrity

To ensure application stability, diagnose system exceptions, and protect the application from unauthorized tampering or exploitation, Giovnex processes non-personal technical telemetry:

  • Hardware Specifications: Device model, display dimensions, total RAM, battery percentage, processor architecture, and operating system version are processed solely to verify device compatibility and optimize rendering pipelines.
  • Security Watchdog & Integrity Verification: The Application monitors execution environments for unauthorized package tampering, root/test-key environments, active debugging bridges (ADB), and virtual machine emulation to prevent malicious manipulation.
  • Error Logging & Diagnostics: When a runtime exception occurs, technical crash diagnostics (error numbers, component identifiers, and device models) are transmitted to a secure backend to facilitate necessary software maintenance and stability updates.
  • Installation Identifier: A non-personal, randomly generated unique identifier (UUID) is assigned locally to manage remote configuration checks and authorized service access.

03 Machine Learning & Voice Processing

Giovnex integrates artificial intelligence and natural language processing interfaces to deliver automated productivity features:

  • Text Inquiries & Summaries: User-submitted text prompts and webpage article summaries are transmitted through encrypted protocols to external neural processing infrastructure to generate relevant responses. Users are advised not to submit sensitive credentials into interactive AI prompt fields.
  • Voice Input: When voice input features are activated, speech recognition is processed locally or through standard operating system voice services. Audio recordings are not preserved, cataloged, or stored on audio-recording database servers.
  • Image Synthesis: Visual generation queries are executed dynamically via external rendering interfaces without storing persistent identifying records.

04 Local Storage, Incognito & Ad-Shield Engine

User browsing information is stored locally on the physical client device and remains entirely under the user's jurisdiction:

  • Client-Side Storage: Bookmarks, browsing records, and custom preferences are stored locally in device-level database storage (TinyDB). They are never mirrored or synced to commercial tracking servers.
  • Incognito Mode: Activating Incognito Mode isolates active browsing sessions and automatically purges cached resources, form inputs, and session cookies upon session termination.
  • Ad-Shield Engine: An integrated script evaluation engine blocks intrusive tracker scripts, malicious redirects, and aggressive pop-ups.

05 Data Subject Rights & Deletion Mechanisms

In accordance with global data protection frameworks (including GDPR, CCPA, and the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012), users maintain complete authority over their stored information:

System Data Purge: Accessible directly within the Application's Settings menu, this function permanently wipes all local browsing records, saved bookmarks, cached data, download histories, and stored session preferences from the device with a single action.

06 Device Permissions & Hardware Access

Giovnex requests specific device permissions strictly when necessary to execute user-directed operations:

  • Storage & Media Access: Utilized strictly to save user-downloaded files, media, and documents into the designated public Downloads directory.
  • Microphone: Utilized exclusively during the active duration of user-initiated voice recognition for search queries or AI interactions.
  • Camera: Utilized exclusively when the user opens the integrated QR / Barcode scanner utility to scan web links.
  • Foreground & Media Service: Utilized to maintain continuous background audio playback while the device screen is locked or idle.

07 Protection of Children's Privacy

Giovnex is a general-purpose browsing and utility application and is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or under the applicable age of digital consent in relevant jurisdictions). The Application does not knowingly collect or solicit personal identifiable information from children. If you believe that a minor has provided information to the Application, please contact the Developer immediately to facilitate prompt removal.
